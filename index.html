TRACK 01 / EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

A rover, finding
its rhythm.

A custom STM32 development board with EtherCAT integration for the SPEAR rover.

KiCadSTM32C++
10 → 1,000 HzMotor control loop rate
100× improvement
Read the liner notes

The challenge: upgrade older microcontroller hardware and move communications from CAN bus to EtherCAT.

My contribution: PCB design and assembly, electrical schematics, C++ programming for wheel control, and manual soldering.

The result: a reusable development board that addressed hardware obsolescence and simplified future EtherCAT prototyping.

TRACK 02 / MATERIALS RESEARCH

What happens
beneath the surface?

Studying how crosslinker concentration and nanoparticle aggregation affect hydrogel behavior.

HydrogelsCompression testingMapping indentation
100+Hydrogel formulations
synthesized and characterized
Read the liner notes

The question: how do composition and nanoparticle aggregation influence a hydrogel’s mechanical properties?

The process: compression tests using the Mach-1 Biomomentum system, stress–strain analysis, and mapping indentation to investigate aggregation.

Beyond the lab: presented the research at FURCA and helped make testing workflows reproducible through documented procedures.
